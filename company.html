About Company

About Modi's Iron Racks

Established in 2001, MODI'S IRON RACKS is a trusted name in the manufacturing and wholesale supply of metal racking, shelving and furniture solutions.

With over two decades of industry experience, we specialise in providing durable, practical and cost-effective storage solutions for commercial, industrial, retail and residential spaces.

Our product range includes slotted angle racks, heavy-duty storage racks, supermarket display racks, wall-mounted shelving systems, metal pipe racks, office furniture and household furniture.

Historical Growth

Milestones in Our 20+ Year Journey

From a local workshop to Bangalore's trusted wholesale metal rack provider.

2001 • FOUNDATION

Establishment in Shivaji Nagar

Commenced direct metal fabrication in Sultan Gunta Road with a focus on slotted angle uprights and heavy bolt-assembled steel shelving.

2008 • COMMERCIAL EXPANSION

Warehouse & Retail Supply

Expanded into large multi-tier warehouse pallet racking, retail supermarket display fixtures, and institutional archive shelving.

2016 • ADVANCED COATINGS

Powder Coating & Tubular Steel Pipe Systems

Introduced electrostatic polymer powder coating for anti-scratch durability, alongside high-capacity cylindrical pipe racks for textile display.

2026 & BEYOND • DIGITAL COMMERCE

Direct Factory Booking

Serving thousands of commercial businesses and residential clients with streamlined digital booking and prompt doorstep delivery across Bengaluru.

Manufacturing Capabilities

Products & Fabrication Services

Comprehensive industrial metal furniture fabrication tailored to custom commercial specifications.

Metal Rack Manufacturing

Precision stamping, punching, bending and welding of heavy-gauge cold rolled steel racks built to precise structural dimensions.

Wholesale Rack Supply

Direct supply to retail businesses, warehouses, institutions, educational facilities, and commercial contractors at factory rates.

Slotted Angle Racks
