Can I inspect assembled rack models in person?

Yes! Our Shivaji Nagar workshop has live display models of standard slotted angle units, wall-mounted brackets, and powder-coated plates so you can test steel thickness and shelf stability before buying.

Do you provide on-site measurement in Bangalore?
